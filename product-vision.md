# Product Vision Statement

- **FOR** individuals, students, employees, and small businesses who regularly use email for personal and professional communication.

- **WHO** need protection against phishing emails, fraudulent links, and email-based cyberattacks but may lack the technical knowledge to identify suspicious messages.

- **THE PhishGuard IS A** cybersecurity-based phishing email detection and awareness platform.

- **THAT** helps users identify potentially malicious emails by analysing email content, suspicious links, and common phishing indicators, providing clear warnings and recommendations to prevent cyber threats.

- **UNLIKE** traditional spam filters and manual email inspection methods, which may fail to identify certain sophisticated phishing attempts or provide sufficient explanations to users.

- **OUR PRODUCT** aims to combine phishing detection with user-friendly explanations and security recommendations, helping users understand potential threats, make safer decisions, and improve their cybersecurity awareness.
